📜 Barbarians (Netflix)

Genre: Historical Drama / Action
Created by: Andreas Heckmann, Arne Nolting, Jan Martin Scharf
Released: 2020

🛡️ Overview
Barbarians is a German historical drama series set during the Roman Empire’s occupation of Germania. It follows three childhood friends — Arminius, Thusnelda, and Folkwin — whose loyalties are tested as they find themselves on opposing sides of a brutal war.

⚔️ Main Characters
Arminius – A Germanic warrior raised by the Romans, torn between two worlds.
Thusnelda – A fierce and intelligent leader who dreams of freeing her people.
Folkwin Wolfspeer – A loyal warrior and friend, driven by honor and love.

🌲 Why I Like This Series
I love Barbarians because of its powerful storytelling, epic battle scenes, and how it brings ancient history to life. The series shows the conflict between loyalty, identity, and freedom — all wrapped in stunning cinematography and authentic performances.

⭐ Favorite Quote
“Freedom must be earned in blood.”
